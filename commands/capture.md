# Capture

Invoke the `vault` skill and capture what was just discovered or decided in
this conversation as a note, following its capture workflow: decide the type
(reusable knowledge, project context, or a daily insight), search for an
existing note first, use the matching template, and tell me the filepath once
written.

If nothing in the recent conversation is actually worth capturing, say so
rather than writing a thin note.
